## Description (English — default language)

```
[h1]🍉 Melon Racer — Get ready to roll![/h1]

[b]No guns. No bullets. Just you, a watermelon, and the track.[/b]

Melon Racer turns Counter-Strike 2 into a kart racer. Each player gets their own physics melon and races it through checkpoints, over ramps and off walls. Race in heats with your friends and see whose melon makes it to the finish line in one piece.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]🏁 How a race works[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Tutorial[/b] — new players start in a short tutorial area to learn how to drive.
[*][b]Hub[/b] — everyone gathers here. Drive around, pick a color for your melon, and step on the start area when you're ready.
[*][b]Countdown[/b] — [i]3… 2… 1… GO![/i] Everyone on the start area joins the heat.
[*][b]Race[/b] — pass every checkpoint in order and finish the laps. Shortcuts that skip a checkpoint don't count!
[*][b]Next track[/b] — a short break, then the next track starts. After the last track everyone goes back to the hub.
[/list]

[h2]🍉 Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Physics melons[/b] — your melon rolls, bounces and flies for real. It's not a car.
[*][b]Melon health[/b] — hard crashes and big landings damage your melon. At zero it [b]breaks apart[/b] and you respawn at your last checkpoint.
[*][b]Wall bounces[/b] — hit a wall at the right angle (about 45°) for a [b]PERFECT[/b] bounce: more speed and no damage. Time a jump on the hit for even more speed. Bounces stack, so there's no top speed.
[*][b]Wall jumps[/b] — push off walls in mid-air to reach shortcuts and save a bad line.
[*][b]Heal zones & teleporters[/b] — patch up your melon and take the fast way around.
[*][b]Paint your melon[/b] — drive over a paint pad in the hub or choose a color in the menu.
[*][b]Multiple tracks[/b] — raced one after another, each with its own number of laps.
[*][b]Custom HUD[/b] — speedometer, checkpoint and lap counter, bounce rating and jump timing.
[/list]

[h2]🎮 Controls[/h2]
[table]
[tr][th]Key[/th][th]Action[/th][/tr]
[tr][td]Mouse[/td][td]Steer (the melon goes where you look)[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]W / S[/td][td]Accelerate / brake[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]A / D[/td][td]Strafe left / right[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Space[/td][td]Jump / wall jump / time a wall bounce[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]E[/td][td]Menu: respawn at checkpoint, back to hub, tutorial, melon color[/td][/tr]
[/table]

[h2]▶ How to play[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Click [b]Subscribe[/b] on this page.
[*]Start CS2 → [b]Play[/b] → [b]Workshop Maps[/b] → [b]Melon Racer[/b].
[*]Playing with friends? They need to subscribe too. Then invite them to your lobby.
[/olist]
[b]Tip:[/b] The first player on the map is the [b]moderator[/b] and can cancel a race that's already running, from the hub start area.

[hr][/hr]

[h2]🚧 Work in progress[/h2]
Melon Racer is still being built. Things will change and there will be bugs. Known gaps right now:
[list]
[*]No 1st/2nd/3rd placing yet — the game only knows when a racer has finished.
[*]If someone gets stuck, the others have to wait until they finish or leave.
[*]No melon sound effects yet.
[/list]
Found a bug or have an idea? Please leave a comment below or open an issue on GitHub.

[h2]🛠 Open source — help build it![/h2]
The whole game mode is open source: scripts, HUD, and a step-by-step guide to building your [b]own tracks in Hammer[/b] (no coding needed).
[url=https://github.com/fluse/cs2-melon-racer]github.com/fluse/cs2-melon-racer[/url]

[i]If you enjoy the melon, please leave a 👍 — it helps other players find the map![/i]
```

[*][b]Heilzonen & Teleporter[/b] — flick deine Melone und nimm den schnellen Weg.
[*][b]Melone anmalen[/b] — fahr im Hub über ein Farbfeld oder wähl eine Farbe im Menü.
[*][b]Mehrere Strecken[/b] — nacheinander gefahren, jede mit eigener Rundenzahl.
[*][b]Eigenes HUD[/b] — Tacho, Checkpoint- und Rundenanzeige, Abpraller-Bewertung und Sprung-Timing.
[/list]
