Get ready to roll!

No guns. No bullets. Just you, a watermelon and the track. Melon Racer turns Counter-Strike 2 into a kart racer: every player gets their own physics melon and races it through checkpoints, over ramps and off walls.

How a race works

  1. Tutorial New players start in a short tutorial area to learn how to drive.
  2. Hub Everyone gathers here. Drive around, pick a color for your melon, and step onto the start area when you're ready.
  3. Countdown 3… 2… 1… GO! Everyone on the start area joins the heat.
  4. Race Pass every checkpoint in order and finish the laps. Shortcuts that skip a checkpoint don't count!
  5. Next track A short break, then the next track starts. After the last one everyone goes back to the hub.

No group? No problem: drive across any track's start line on your own and the clock starts — that's a time trial. Your best time on every track is saved.

Features

Physics melons

Your melon rolls, bounces and flies for real. It's not a car.

Melon health

Hard crashes and big landings damage your melon. At zero it breaks apart and you respawn at your last checkpoint.

Wall bounces

Hit a wall at about 45° for a PERFECT bounce: more speed and no damage. Time a jump on the hit for even more. Bounces stack — there's no top speed.

Boost — if you dare

Hold the left mouse button for extra speed and a glowing trail. It costs health while you hold it. Too long and your melon breaks!

Momentum

Hit your top speed again and again without slowing down too much, and your top speed keeps growing.

Wall jumps

Push off walls in mid-air to reach shortcuts or save a bad line. Three charges that refill over time — a PERFECT one leaves faster than you came in.

Jump pads & lift shafts

Press jump on a glowing pad to launch high and fast, or bounce between the walls of a shaft to climb up.

Heal zones, teleporters & water

Patch up your melon, take the fast way around — and better jump over the water: it eats all your speed.

Paint your melon

Drive over a paint pad or choose a color in the menu. Your melon glows in its color, so everyone sees who's who.

Time trial

Every run is timed, alone or in a heat. Beat your best and you get a gold NEW BEST. Messed up the start? Press R.

Multiple tracks

Loops with several laps or A-to-B tracks, raced one after another in a heat.

Custom HUD

Speedometer with health bar and wall-jump charges, run clock, checkpoint strip, lap counter, bounce rating and jump timing.

How to play

  1. Subscribe to Melon Racer on the Steam Workshop.
  2. Start CS2 → Play → Workshop Maps → Melon Racer.
  3. Playing with friends? They need to subscribe too — then invite them to your lobby.

The first player on the map is the moderator and can cancel a running race from the hub's start area.

Open source — build your own track

The whole game mode is open source: the cs_script gameplay scripts, the HUD, and the tools around them. Tracks are pure Hammer work — triggers named by convention, no coding needed.

  • Track creation guide — step by step in Hammer
  • Mapping API — every entity name and script input
  • Game design — how every mechanic works
  • Issues — bug reports and ideas welcome

Work in progress

Melon Racer is still being built. Things will change and there will be bugs. Known gaps right now:

  • No 1st/2nd/3rd placing yet — the game only knows when a racer has finished.
  • If someone gets stuck, the others have to wait until they finish or leave.
  • No melon sound effects yet.
